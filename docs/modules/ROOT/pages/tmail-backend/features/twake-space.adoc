= Twake Space
:navtitle: Twake Space

Give every TwakeSpace space its own team mailbox!

The Twake Space extension follows the spaces of TwakeSpace, and gives each of them a
xref:tmail-backend/features/teamMailboxes.adoc[team mailbox]:

- A space gets a team mailbox, with an address derived from its name, as soon as TMail hears about it.
- Space admins become managers of the team mailbox, space editors become members. Viewers get no access, as team
mailboxes have no read only access.
- The team mailbox follows the members of the space: additions, removals and role changes.
- The mail the team mailbox receives and sends is reported to the space feed.
- A deleted space loses access to its team mailbox at once. The team mailbox is deleted 30 days later.

It replaces the Twake Mail side service, with no storage of its own.

See xref:tmail-backend/configure/twake-space.adoc[this page for configuring the extension].

== How it works

TMail talks with the rest of the Twake platform through RabbitMQ only:

[source]
----
ldap-rest   --- space: twake.space.* ---------------------->  +-------+
admin panel --- admin-panel: dns.validated ----------------->  | TMail |  --- activity: com.twake.mail.* ---> TwakeSpace
ldap-rest   <-- space: twake.space.sync.requested -----------  +-------+
----

Each event is a hint that a space changed. TMail applies what the event carries to the team mailbox of the space:

|===
| Event | Effect on the team mailbox

| `twake.space.created`
| Created when the space has none, then its members are set.

| `twake.space.synced`
| Same as created. The members become exactly those of the event: anyone else, including members added by hand in
TMail, is removed. The `provisioned` event is published again.

| `twake.space.member.added`, `twake.space.member.role.changed`
| The named members are added, or get their new role. A member who becomes viewer loses access.

| `twake.space.member.removed`
| The named members are removed.

| `twake.space.updated`
| Nothing: a team mailbox keeps its address when its space is renamed.

| `twake.space.deleted`
| Closed: every member is removed. The team mailbox and its mail stay until the purge.

| `twake.space.sync.completed`
| The live spaces of the organization that the snapshot does not list are closed, as on a deletion.

| `dns.validated`
| When the mail DNS of the organization is validated, TMail asks ldap-rest for a snapshot of its spaces
(`twake.space.sync.requested`), which provisions the spaces created before.
|===

Members are matched by email, case insensitive. Fields of the events that are not listed in the
xref:tmail-backend/configure/twake-space.adoc#_consumed_events[event reference] are ignored.

=== Addresses

The address of a team mailbox comes from the name of its space, the same way the Twake Mail side service did:

. Accents are dropped and the name is lowercased.
. Every run of characters other than `a-z`, `0-9`, `-` and `_` becomes a single `-`.
. Dashes are trimmed from both ends, and the result is cut to 64 characters. An empty result becomes `space`.
. The optional `teamMailbox.prefix` is put first, like `space-` for `space-sales@acme.com`.

`Équipe Ventes` in organization `acme.com` thus gets `equipe-ventes@acme.com`.

When the address is already held by another space, a user, an alias, a group or, in LDAP deployments, an LDAP mailing
list, `-2`, `-3`... up to `-20` are tried in turn.

A team mailbox no space owns at that address is adopted by the space: where there are spaces, team mailboxes are only
created for them, so such a team mailbox is what a crash between its creation and the writing of its annotations left.
The space completes its folders, binds it and sets its members.

A space never gets a second team mailbox, even after a rename.

=== Storage

The extension adds no table and no index. The space a team mailbox belongs to is stored as
link:https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5464[RFC 5464] annotations of the team mailbox's root mailbox:

|===
| Key | Value | Written

| `/shared/vendor/twake/organizationId` | the organization id | when the team mailbox is created
| `/shared/vendor/twake/spaceId` | the space id | when the team mailbox is created
| `/shared/vendor/twake/deletedAt` | ISO 8601 instant of the deletion | when the space is deleted
|===

A team mailbox without `spaceId` was not made for a space, and is never taken over on its own.

These keys are read only. Members can read them with IMAP `GETMETADATA`, but IMAP `SETMETADATA`, and any annotation
update through the mailbox manager, is refused for them, whoever asks.

Everything else is read from where it already lives: members are the team mailbox ACLs, the team mailbox of a space is
found by scanning the team mailboxes of its domain. A domain holds a few dozen spaces, so the scan stays cheap.

=== Mail activity

When a message is delivered into a team mailbox, or added to its `Sent` folder, TMail publishes
`com.twake.mail.message.received.v1` or `com.twake.mail.message.sent.v1` on the `activity` exchange. The event names
the message and its subject, and points to the root mailbox announced at provisioning, from which TwakeSpace builds the
link that opens it in Twake Mail.

Only team mailboxes bound to a live space report: the mail of a closed space, or of a team mailbox no space owns, is not
reported. The events carry no sender and no preview, since space viewers have no access to the mailbox.

=== Deletion

A deleted space loses its members at once. Its team mailbox keeps its mail and its address, so no other space takes the
address in the meantime. Mail sent to the address is still delivered, but nobody can read it.

A deleted space stays deleted: a later creation, sync, member event or mail about it changes nothing.

After the retention (30 days by default), the purge deletes the team mailbox and its mail, which frees the address.

== Guarantees

Delivery is *at least once*, with no deduplication on TMail's side:

- Every handler is idempotent: handling an event twice gives the same team mailbox, with the same members.
- Every published event carries a deterministic id, as CloudEvent `id` and AMQP message id, so a republished event
keeps the id of the first copy. TwakeSpace deduplicates on it.
- `com.twake.mail.space.provisioned.v1` is published once the team mailbox exists and its annotations are written, so
it always comes before the mail events of that team mailbox.
- The events of the queue are handled one at a time, by a single consumer across the cluster, in the order they were
published.

== Limits

- Space events have to carry `organizationDomain`: TMail finds the team mailbox of a space among the team mailboxes of
its domain. Recent ldap-rest versions do. A `twake.space.deleted` without it scans every team mailbox.
- An event about a domain TMail does not have yet is skipped. The DNS validation of the organization requests a sync,
which brings its spaces back.
- Team mailboxes are not to be created by other means on a domain with spaces: a space adopts the team mailbox no
space owns that sits at its address, and makes its members those of the space.
- `twake.space.sync.completed` closes every unlisted space of the organization, including one created between the
snapshot and the event. Its next event does not reopen it: an operator binds it again.
- `domain.user.deleted` is not handled by this extension: TMail's user data deletion, which the Twake Workplace user
deletion consumer triggers, already revokes the rights of the deleted user on the mailboxes shared with them, team
mailboxes included.
